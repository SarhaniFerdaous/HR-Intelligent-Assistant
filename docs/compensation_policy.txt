ACME Corp — Compensation & Benefits Policy

Salary Payment: Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month via direct bank transfer.
Salary Review: Annual salary reviews take place every January based on performance ratings.
Performance Bonus: Employees rated "Exceeds Expectations" receive a bonus of 10-15% of annual salary.
Health Insurance: Full medical and dental coverage provided for employee and immediate family from day 1.
Transport Allowance: Monthly transport allowance of 150 TND for employees commuting more than 20km.
Meal Vouchers: Daily meal vouchers of 8 TND per working day.
Training Budget: Each employee has an annual training budget of 1200 TND for professional development.
Remote Work: Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week after probation completion.
Equipment: Laptop and peripherals provided. Mobile phone provided for manager level and above.
Overtime: Overtime is compensated at 1.5x hourly rate for weekdays, 2x for weekends.
