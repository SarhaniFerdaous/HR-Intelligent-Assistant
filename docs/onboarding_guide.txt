ACME Corp — New Employee Onboarding Guide

Week 1:
- Day 1: HR orientation, ID badge, laptop setup, system access
- Day 2: Meet your team and direct manager
- Day 3-5: Department-specific training begins

Required Documents on Day 1:
- National ID or Passport
- Tax registration number
- Bank account details for payroll
- Signed employment contract

IT Setup:
- Laptops are pre-configured by the IT department
- Request additional software via the IT portal within 5 business days
- VPN access is granted after security training completion

Probation Period:
- All new employees undergo a 3-month probation period
- Performance review occurs at the end of month 2
- Permanent contract issued upon successful completion

Dress Code:
- Business casual Monday to Thursday
- Casual Friday policy applies
- Client-facing meetings require formal attire
